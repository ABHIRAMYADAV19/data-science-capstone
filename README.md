# Consumer vs. Business AI Usage — Anthropic Economic Index

## Research Question

**Do consumers and businesses use AI differently on the same tasks?** 

## Dataset

**The Anthropic Economic Index** — aggregated, privacy-preserving statistics on how people use Claude, broken out by platform (consumer Claude.ai vs. business/enterprise API), task (O\*NET / SOC occupation), geography, and interaction pattern.

- **Source:** https://huggingface.co/datasets/Anthropic/EconomicIndex
- **Methodology paper:** Handa, K. et al. (2025). *Which Economic Tasks are Performed with AI? Evidence from Millions of Claude Conversations.* [arXiv:2503.04761](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.04761)
